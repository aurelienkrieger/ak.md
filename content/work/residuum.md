# Residuum

Residuum is a light art installation created by Paul Marlier and exhibited in August and September 2016 at Espace Ecureuil, Foundation for contemporary art, in Toulouse, France. On this occasion, I supported the artists with technical consulting for the light fixture, and custom software development for the 3D mapping of the light animation with video contents.
